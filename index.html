Our founder

Michelle Mitchell's journey to becoming the Chief Executive of Cancer Research UK is a testament to her dedication to public service and her expertise in the charity sector. Her career began with the National Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Children (NSPCC) and progressed through various roles at Age UK, the Multiple Sclerosis Society of Great Britain, and the Fawcett Society.
